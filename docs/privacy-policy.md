# Ambr privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Ambr keeps receipts, warranty cards and warranty dates for the things you own. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What Ambr stores, and where

- Items you add, their photos (receipts, warranty cards), dates, prices, serial numbers, notes and service history are stored **only on your phone**.
- Ambr has no account and no server. We, the makers of Ambr, cannot see your items.

## Camera and photos

Ambr uses the camera only when you photograph a receipt, a warranty card or a serial barcode. Photos you pick from your gallery or PDFs you choose are copied into the app. Reading the text and barcodes happens on your phone, using Google ML Kit, without sending the images anywhere.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, Ambr saves a copy of your items and photos to a private app folder in **your own Google Drive** (the `drive.appdata` space). Only Ambr on your devices can open that folder. Ambr asks Google for your email address only to show which account is used. You can turn backup off at any time in the app, and delete the backup from Google Drive settings → Manage apps.

## Purchases

Ambr Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Ambr only learns whether your Google account owns Ambr Pro.

## Notifications

Ambr shows at most two reminders before a warranty ends. They are created on your phone.

## Sharing

Ambr does not sell, rent or share your information, and has no ads or analytics. Information leaves your phone only when you choose to share a receipt or PDF, or turn on Google Drive backup.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any item, or all data at once (You → About → Delete all my data). Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

## Children

Ambr is not directed at children under 13.
