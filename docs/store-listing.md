## Full description
Your AC stopped cooling. Where's the receipt? When does the warranty end? Which service center?

Ambr keeps the proof for everything you own, and finds it in two taps when something breaks.

SAVE IN 15 SECONDS
• Snap the receipt or warranty card. Ambr reads the store, price, date and warranty for you.
• Scan the serial number barcode from the box or sticker.
• Add a PDF invoice or a photo from your gallery.

ALWAYS KNOW WHAT'S COVERED
• See every item's warranty at a glance: covered, ending soon or expired.
• Track extra coverage too, like a 10-year compressor warranty or free service visits.
• Get a reminder 30 days and 7 days before a warranty ends, while repairs are still free. Never more than two.

WHEN SOMETHING BREAKS
• Tap "Something broke?" and pick the item.
• The claim pack shows the receipt, serial number, purchase date and service center in one place.
• Call the service center, share a PDF, or show the receipt at the counter at full brightness.
• Log each repair to build a service history.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads.
• Everything stays on your phone. Receipt reading happens on the device, even offline.
• Optional backup to your own Google Drive.
• Optional app lock with PIN or fingerprint.

FREE AND PRO
Free: up to 10 items with every feature you need to claim.

Works with receipts from Walton, Singer, Samsung, Gree, Vision, Star Tech, Ryans and more.
