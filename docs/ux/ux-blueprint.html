2 · What changed from the spec

Nine UX decisions

The spec is the base. These changes come from walking each moment as Abba and Nusrat.

  1. “Something broke?” button on HomeSearch assumes typing and spelling. A big button opens a photo grid of everything owned instead.
  2. The breakdown path lands on the claim packFrom “Something broke?” the answer and the proof are one tap away. Item detail stays the route from Vault and Search.
  3. “No receipt? Type it in” on the cameraNot every purchase has paper at hand. Nobody gets stuck in front of a camera.
  4. Second coverage lives in a sheetMost items have one warranty. Compressor 10 years or 4 free services goes in a sheet, so Confirm stays short.
  5. Duplicate check is a dialogOne question, two answers, before saving. Never a separate screen.
  6. Restore comes before WelcomeShown only when a Drive backup exists, so a new phone never means retyping 23 items.
  7. The free limit never touches a claimItem 11 opens the Pro sheet. Every saved item keeps its claim pack, share and call.
  8. Reminder permission after the first saveAt that moment the reason is concrete: “We'll remind you on 22 Sep.”
  9. Archive before Delete“Sold or gave away” keeps the receipt as resale proof. Delete stays in the overflow menu.

Rules carried into the visual design

  • Light mode only. Status pills always pair color with a word and an icon.
  • Public Sans. Body at least 16 sp; the status sentence on Item detail and Claim pack at 24 sp or larger. Layouts reflow at 200% font scale.
  • Tap targets at least 48 × 48 dp. Primary actions full width at the bottom, within thumb reach. One primary action per screen.
  • Dates as “14 Mar 2027”, never 14/03/27. Money as ৳4,85,000 with South Asian grouping.
  • Amber on white only for large text or with a dark outline, to meet WCAG AA.
